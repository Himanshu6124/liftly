# Exercise data attribution

Exercise names, instructions, difficulty levels and images in `files/exercises.json` and
`files/exercises/` are derived from **free-exercise-db** by yuhonas
(https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into the public domain under the
Unlicense:

> This is free and unencumbered software released into the public domain.
> Anyone is free to copy, modify, publish, use, compile, sell, or distribute this software,
> either in source code form or as a compiled binary, for any purpose, commercial or
> non-commercial, and by any means.

Aliases, form cues, common mistakes and the muscle mapping were written for Liftly
(`tools/seed/curation.py`). Images were downscaled to 360 px.
